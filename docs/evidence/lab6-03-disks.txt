  Predictions recorded before the test: (1) a snapshot can be taken with the disk attached and mounted; (2) after expansion the guest sees 8 GiB but the filesystem stays 4 GiB until grown; (3) the restored disk is 4 GiB, its checksum matches, and after-snapshot.txt is absent. All three held.

The snapshot and the restored disk, created through their full create forms with Networking set, are DenyAll / publicNetworkAccess Disabled. Remediated in M4.
